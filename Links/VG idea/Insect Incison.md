Summary:
An open-world survial rpg is about a scavenger/alien that ends up in a hollow asteroid, which holds a cavern infested with insects, crustaceans, and mollusks. Without thumbs, the creature relies on the substance it can regurgitate to alter the organisms around them to suit their situation. The evenutal goal, besides taking out the megafuana, would be finding some way to escape the massive entrance at the center of the map. 


Playthrough:
the player would find themselves under a huge hole, in which a vast cavern surronds them. The first objective would be to find food, either by taking out small critters or by stealing eggs. Once satiated, the player would steal an egg to begin their biopunk factory. The player would go on to graft and breed insects to properly automate things, occasionally taking out the megafauna. The eventual goal would be to assemble a creature so great it can take on the weird thing thats blocking the entrance of the asteroid.


Gimmick:
Instead of the conventional pipeline, where in the player gets new matierials and such to upgrade themselves and different equipment, the player has to breed and graft a horde of insects in order to achieve new tools, vehicles, and automations. The main features are:
- Using biomass, the player can cover eggs into imprinting onto them, and any subsequent children will also imprint on the player.
- If an animal has a segment removed, like a leg or it's split in half, biomass can be used as a glue, so it can either repair the animal, or you can graft segments to it.
- Grafted animals inherit traits associated with the body parts it carries, such as the horns of a rhinceros beetle or the gun of a pistol shrimp. These get implmented into their DNA, which will then get shared with their kids.
