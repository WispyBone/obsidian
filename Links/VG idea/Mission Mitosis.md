Basic idea is that a blob creature is bioengineered to take care of parasitic planets getting too close to earth. The blob creature is like cubivore/spore in that it absorbs the DNA of whatever animals it eats and grows certain mutations from it. The difference is that, with sufficent enough food, the blob can under go mitosis. The goal of the game is to conquer the planet with the blob minions and take out the main parasite that lives in the center of the planet. 

Background:
The setting is that earth realized that moon sized orbitals are appearing in the solar system, and upon further inspection, were parasitic in nature, and could potentially come to earth and eradicate everything. Humanity saw that life existed in these planets, possible because (*insert reason here*),  so they decided the best course of action was to bioengineer an amoeba like creature that can its own genelogy. The amoeba tiny brain is then given enough information about animals and its purpose to succeed in the mission, and blasted to whatever planet earth deems is necessary.

Playthrough:
In a procedurally generated setting, the blob creature wakes up in their space capsule. The map is kind of like DST, where there are a couple of main continents and some islands scattered about. The different continets are home to different biomes, conditons, and animals, with one island being the entrance to the main parasite. The main parasite is accesible upon the point the player conquers sea travel, but its recommended to explore the world and get more upgrades before facing the final boss. The blob then goes through the early-game, where they kill the starting animals and amass a sizable army of minions. Once the starting continet is finished, they can unlock sea travel via the beaches and travel to the other parts, growing their dna collection. They splinter more and more of there minions, creating infrastructure and armies, up until they feel ready to conquer the final boss. While they could continue playing, its like terraria where theres no real point to it besides achievement hunting.

Gimmick
The blob mechanic works as follows:
- There are two kinds of blobs, the alpha and the pack (lack of a better distinction)
- The alpha is different in that it can manuallly change its dna, where as the pack can only progress from the state in which they mitosis, adding on to what they were. However, in comparison to the pack, the alpha has significantly weaker mutations and fewer possible mutations, leading to a need for the packs. 
- Upon death of the alpha, the player picks a pack to turn into the Alpha, severly downsizing the traits they had to match the equivilent for what an alpha can have.
- Alphas can either have the packs do miscllanous things in the world, like follow or do certain directions, or have them in their inventory, depending on what traits are posseded
-  The packs are meant to be special. They aren't like pikmin where theres tons of replacable faces, mainly because of lag concerns. Instead, pack blobs are meant to be amassed like a party, creating different mitosis when needed and growing with the alpha. the way to limit this is with food, as the games only form of agriculture is a chemical eating trait, meaning the player has to wait until surfing becomes available to do any major experimentation, and even so, if they want easy food, thats an entire trait just to provide sulf sustenance.
- The packs function like that one Warhammer creature about peak evolution (kroot). Given enough  animal parts, like moose horns or shark jaws, they transform into such traits. 
- Packs are locked with the certain trait they replicated with, but they can be fed other animals to gain their genetic data. The caveat is that while they are extremly malleable, the more they want to spec into one trait, the less they can cover for the rest, eventully reaching an evolutionary dead end. Essentially, while you can mix and match traits in the beginning, once certain thresholds are met, the traits are locked, and eventully you cant edit the pack anymore. One this happens, they are then treated as a different species, allowing for new genetic variation if consumed by another pack and breeding within other pack species with similar trait spreads.
- Packs can be removed in three ways. Either it starves, its eaten by another pack, or its eaten by the alpha. Besides starvation, the other two serve opposite purposes. When eaten by the alpha, it provides half the hunger the cumilative food and replication would have provided, along with provided all the gene data that it had, making it good for removal and a pocket ability. Conversely, having packs eat eachother make a good way to build off of previous dead ends, as they are treated like sperate species, allowing for either duplication or branching off into different paths, like a gun pack divering int a m16 and shotgun

Ecosystem:
The animals would be seperated into major classifications, mammals, birds, mollusks, and then diverge into individual species to determine what traits are possible for the animals and to limit what evoultions the pack can do. It functions as a sort of skill tree, with ~6 major paths with little branches diverging out. Many will have same traits in similiar positons, showing convergent evolution and allowing for more diversification in certain enviorments, like a planet without insects. Each playthrough would have a randomly generated ecosystem per environment, with a single cohesion element shared between all biomes depending on the planetary conditions. With the random generation, players cant beline for certain met traits, as each new planet and even each biome contain certain niches that cant be explored, such as a planet not having birds, so the players flight infrastructure would come from insects or bats.

Infrastructure:
the main prupose for the blobs is to not only act like palworld pals, either fighting with you or being used as a weapon, but also as vehicles an workstaions in create. Given certain traits, like some appartus to allow for loud noises, automation can be made between the different blobs, allowing for the culitvtion and spread of a certain species across the continents. The end goal would be that the player would have developed different farms and networks to craft amazing tools and blobs of destruction to conquer bosses and prepare for the parasite.
